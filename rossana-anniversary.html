Today

Dear Rossana,

A year ago today, I didn't know how much room you were about to take up in my life, in the best way possible. I remember being a little nervous, a little unsure of how things would go, and mostly just hoping I wouldn't say something dumb. Look at us now, three hundred and sixty five days in.

It's strange how a year can feel both like no time at all and like you've always been around. I can barely picture what my routine looked like before you were part of it. Somewhere along the way you turned into the person I want to tell things to first, the good news and the bad, the boring middle-of-the-day stuff too.

You have this way of making ordinary days feel worth remembering. A random Tuesday text, the way you laugh at your own jokes before you finish telling them, the way you check on me without making it a big deal. None of it has gone unnoticed. I've been paying attention the whole time, even when I didn't say so.

I think what gets me most is how steady you are. Not boring, never that, but dependable in a way that makes everything else feel a little less shaky. I know where I stand with you, and that has quietly made me braver in other parts of my life too.

I'm grateful for the version of me that gets to know you. Softer, steadier, more willing to say what I actually mean instead of dancing around it. You had a hand in that, even on days you probably didn't realize it.

I don't know exactly what the next year looks like, and honestly that's part of what makes it exciting. New routines, new inside jokes, probably a few more arguments about what to watch. Whatever it is, I want to be figuring it out next to you.

So here's to the year behind us, messy and good in equal measure, and to however many more we get to build.

Yours, always finding new reasons,
